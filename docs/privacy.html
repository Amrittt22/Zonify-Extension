Zonify — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Zonify is a Pomodoro timer and site blocker for Chrome. It runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what data Zonify stores, when (and only when) it sends anything over the network, and how you can delete it.

Short version: Zonify makes no network requests at all unless you turn on the optional AI insights feature. With insights off, nothing leaves your machine.

1. Data stored on your device

Zonify stores all of its data in chrome.storage.local on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server by the extension itself. The data includes:

  • Settings — focus/break durations, your blocklist, and whether AI insights are enabled.
  • Timer state — current phase, end time, paused time remaining.
  • Session history — for focus sessions: a start timestamp, duration, and whether the session ended normally or early. Used to compute streaks and daily totals.
  • Statistics — sessions completed, sessions started but ended early, total focus minutes, streak days, focus minutes by day of week (Mon–Sun), focus minutes by time-of-day bucket (morning / afternoon / evening), and per-domain blocked-attempt counts.
  • Insights cache — if you use AI insights, the most recent generated summary and the week it belongs to, plus a randomly generated install ID used only for fair-use rate limiting.

Zonify does not read page content, form input, or browsing history. The <all_urls> host permission exists solely so declarativeNetRequest can block the sites on your blocklist while a focus session is running.

2. Optional AI insights (the only network feature)

AI insights are off by default. When you enable them and click Generate insight, Zonify sends one request to the backend URL you configured. The request contains only aggregate weekly numbers:

FieldWhat it is
installIdA random UUID generated once on your device, used only for fair-use rate limiting. Not linked to your identity.
weekA human-readable label for the week (e.g. "Oct 13 - Oct 19").
totalFocusMinutesTotal focus minutes for the week.
sessionsCompletedNumber of focus sessions completed.
sessionsStartedButEndedEarlyNumber of sessions you stopped before finishing.
streakDaysYour current daily streak.
minutesByDayFocus minutes per day of week (Mon–Sun).
minutesByHourBucketFocus minutes by time of day: morning, afternoon, evening.
topBlockedDomainsYour top 3 blocked sites by attempt count, with counts (e.g. youtube.com, 84). Domains are normalized and must pass a hostname check; entries that are not valid public hostnames (such as localhost) are dropped.
previousWeekFocusMinutesPrior week's focus minutes, or null if there were none.

Never sent: URLs you visited, page titles, browsing history, timestamps, individual session records, your full blocklist, or any content from pages you viewed. The backend's request schema is strict and rejects any additional field.

Test connection sends a single GET /health request with no data.

The backend you point Zonify at is your choice. The reference backend forwards the aggregate summary above to an LLM provider (Anthropic by default) to generate the short summary you see. If you self-host the backend, only you (and whichever provider you configure) see that data. Please review the privacy policy of any backend and provider you use.

3. Permissions

  • storage — save settings, timer state, sessions, and stats locally.
  • alarms — drive the timer so it survives closing the popup.
  • notifications — one notification when a focus session completes.
  • declarativeNetRequest — block sites on your blocklist during focus sessions.
  • <all_urls> (host permission) — required scope for the blocking rules above.

4. Third parties

Zonify contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. With AI insights disabled, it makes no network requests at all. With insights enabled, data flows only to the backend URL you configured and onward to the LLM provider that backend uses. Zonify itself does not sell or share your data for advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • All extension data lives in chrome.storage.local on your device until you uninstall Zonify or clear the extension's storage.
  • The backend is stateless with respect to your content: it does not store your summaries. It keeps only short-lived, in-memory rate-limit counters (keyed by IP and install ID) to protect the service; these reset automatically.
  • You can turn AI insights off at any time in Timer → AI insights. No further requests are made; the cached insight remains until it is overwritten or cleared with your other data.

6. Children

Zonify is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the extension and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the project's release notes.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be raised through the project's issue tracker (linked from the repository's README) or via the contact email listed in the Chrome Web Store listing for Zonify.
